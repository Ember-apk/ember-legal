How It Works

The anatomy of
a burned message.

Key Generation
Your device generates a Curve25519 key pair at registration. The private key never leaves your phone.
Blind Transmission
Only the destination is visible to the server. No sender field. The payload is encrypted — opaque to everyone else.
Push & Forget
The server pushes the encrypted payload and forgets it. No storage. No logs. No record of what was sent.
Decrypt & Burn
The recipient decrypts locally. The timer starts. When it expires — the message is gone. Only ash.
